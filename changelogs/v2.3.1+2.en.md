## Changelog
 - Fix downloaded videos not playing
 - Fix downloads failing at once when the video title contains a colon
 - Fix "Re-download" doing nothing for downloads that failed early
 - Keep the downloaded size on display while a download is paused
 - Accelerated downloads and playback (experimental): a connection that stalls is retried instead of failing the download

## Attention
 - Only the Android app is maintained for now. The other platforms still use the original project's code and have not been tested.
 - With accelerated downloads on, keep the app open until downloads finish.
