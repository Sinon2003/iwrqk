## Changelog
 - Accelerated downloads and playback (experimental) now start on one connection and split into parallel parts only when a short trial is faster: no longer slower than a direct connection where parallel parts do not help, and playback starts sooner
 - Auto quality picks the starting resolution from the speed of recent playback and the video's real bitrate; it starts at 540 when there is nothing recent
 - The player keeps a bounded cache in memory (about 30 seconds ahead), which downloads less ahead of playback; seeking far back loads again
 - Network errors now say what is wrong instead of showing an exception: whether the network or proxy is down or only Iwara is out of reach, and why
 - The update dialog shows the changelog in the app's language

## Attention
 - Only the Android app is maintained.
