## Changelog
 - The tag filter and the blocked-tag picker have a catalogue under the input: all tags in twelve groups such as Common, Sex acts, Kinks, Outfits & items, Series and Characters; tap a tag to pick it and again to drop it. The catalogue ships with the app, so nothing is loaded
 - In landscape the player's options open at the side instead of covering most of the video from below; quality, playback speed and aspect ratio are in one panel and apply on a tap. The speed and quality buttons of the top bar open the same panel
 - The title of the history page is the switch between Cloud history and Device history; it no longer takes the room of a button, and the buttons beside it no longer cut the title off
 - Searching this device's history finds as you type, says what to type before you do, and tells you when nothing matches
 - Changing the name or the profile description says "Updated"
 - Corrected the Chinese names of two tags

## Attention
 - Only the Android app is maintained; the Windows, macOS, Linux and web projects have been removed from the repository.
 - Tag names are offered in the Simplified and Traditional Chinese interface only; filters and blocked tags still work by the original tags.
 - The cloud history cannot be searched: the site offers no search for it.
