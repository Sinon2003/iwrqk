## Changelog
 - Tags can be shown by their Chinese names: all of the site's 3000-odd tags have a Simplified and a Traditional Chinese name, shown on videos, in the filter and among the blocked tags; turn it off under App settings → 标签显示中文
 - The tag filter and the blocked-tag picker find tags by typing Chinese, and list each suggestion with its name and its tag
 - Fixed the blocked-tag picker showing no suggestions and not keeping the chosen tags
 - New Playback preload setting: 30 seconds (default), 1, 3 or 10 minutes, or the full video; the full video uses temporary storage that is cleared when the video closes
 - Login and the token request share a 20 second limit; after a timeout or closing the dialog a retry starts at once instead of waiting for the attempt that hung
 - Automatic quality also learns the speed of a fast connection that fills the buffer at once, and no longer forgets it five minutes into a long video
 - Accelerated downloads and playback (experimental) no longer counts the slow start when judging whether parallel transfer is faster

## Attention
 - Only the Android app is maintained.
 - Tag names are offered in the Simplified and Traditional Chinese interface only; filters and blocked tags still work by the original tags.
